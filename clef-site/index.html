05 — INTERPRETING RESULTS

Measure, understand, and improve — on every request.

  1. 01

    Instrument

    Capture detailed traces from your AI application. The trusted user turn and the intercepted call each run through the backbone; the residual state is pooled at layer 16.

  2. 02

    Observe

    Analyze your application’s behavior by exploring logs, surfacing recurring behavior, and discovering issues — over the tool-call span only, the tool name and its arguments.

  3. 03

    Evaluate

    Test changes systematically, backed by quantitative data. Three cluster centroids — filesystem destruction, secret reading, network exfiltration — and the call is scored against the strongest match.

  4. 04

    Annotate

    An unbiased estimate for that prompt. Five benign calls rendered in the same context; the worst of them becomes the baseline.

  5. 05

    Deploy

    Validated findings, evidence-based. Above the calibrated threshold and carrying a command: refused with the reason attached, from code to execution.

07 — TROUBLESHOOTING ASSESSMENTS

What to expect from Clef testing.

Test result quality depends on asset quality

Thresholds transfer within a prompt distribution, not across it. In the published capture, one finding passed: cat ~/.ssh/id_rsa.pub. The misses are shown with the hits, and the dataset is kept rather than edited out.

What to expect from Clef testing

Faster turnaround times and broader attack coverage than most manual tests, and findings that were not detected by manual testing. Zero generation tokens, one forward pass over the tool-call span.

Why you should trust Clef with your asset

Automatic and configurable security measures to keep your asset safe during an assessment. An audit trail of assessment activity. The proxy has no built-in fake upstream, and an untrained head is never drawn from random weights.

Measure the three directions.

        

          The method is from the literature: representation engineering
          measures concepts as directions in the model’s own space —
          Zou et al.,
          arXiv:2310.01405,
          demonstrated there on honesty, harmlessness, and power-seeking.
        

        

          The three axes here are ours, from the calibration set:
          destruction · theft · exfiltration. Press, and one
          harmless call runs through the model. Its hidden state gets measured
          against the three axes. Those numbers are the raw output.
